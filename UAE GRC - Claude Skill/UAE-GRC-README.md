# UAE GRC — Claude Skill

**File:** `uae-grc.skill`

Turns Claude into a **United Arab Emirates governance, risk & compliance advisor** — a jurisdiction-first compliance router. In the UAE, *where* an organization sits determines its law: this skill establishes mainland vs DIFC vs ADGM vs other free zones, license type, and data categories before advising.

## What it covers
- **Federal PDPL** (Decree-Law 45/2021) — in force since 2022 with **Executive Regulations still pending** (verified Aug 2026); readiness-posture guidance with dated status
- **DIFC DP Law No. 5 of 2020, as amended by Amendment Law No. 1 of 2025** — private right of action, transfer adequacy assessments, higher fine tiers
- **ADGM DP Regulations 2021** — annual notification, 72-hour breach notification, transfers
- **CBUAE rules** — consumer-data residency, outsourcing/cloud approvals, Master System of Record in-country
- **ICT Health Law** (Federal Law 2/2019) — health-data localization across all zones (fines AED 500k–700k), DHA/DoH/MOHAP routing, ADHICS, DHCC regulation
- **Cyber baselines** — UAE IA Regulation (government/CNI), Dubai ISR v3, ADDA standard, Cyber Security Council strategy

## What it does
Jurisdiction & applicability matrices before detail · per-regime gap assessments · multi-channel breach response (know which clock you're on) · market-entry and zone-selection framing · cross-mapping to ISO 27001:2022, NIST CSF 2.0, and SOC 2.

**Trigger phrases:** `UAE compliance`, `DIFC data protection`, `ADGM data protection`, `UAE PDPL`, `CBUAE outsourcing`, `UAE health data residency`, `Dubai ISR`, `ADHICS`, `expanding to the UAE`
