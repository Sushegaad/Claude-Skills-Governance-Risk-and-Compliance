# ISO 27001:2022 vs. TISAX — Do You Still Need It for Your VW Contract?

Short answer: **yes, you still need TISAX.** ISO 27001 certification is a strong head start, but it does not substitute for TISAX, and VW (like other OEMs) will require the TISAX label/assessment result on the ENX platform regardless of your ISO certificate.

## Why ISO 27001 doesn't substitute for TISAX

TISAX is **an assessment with shareable labels, not a certification** — governed by the ENX Association on behalf of the VDA. It's built on ISO 27001, with roughly **70–80% overlap** between the VDA ISA Information Security catalogue and ISO 27001:2022 Annex A / ISMS clauses (policies, HR security, physical security, access control, cryptography, operations security, supplier management, incident management, continuity). But several things are TISAX-specific and not covered by your ISO 27001 certificate:

| Gap | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Fixed maturity targets + cutback scoring** | ISO 27001 lets you tailor controls via a Statement of Applicability. TISAX requires every applicable question to reach a target maturity of **3 ("Established")** on a 0–5 scale, with **cutback rules** that prevent strong scores elsewhere from offsetting weak ones. |
| **Prototype Protection module** | Physical/organizational prototype security has no ISO 27001 equivalent (only relevant if your VW objectives touch prototype parts/vehicles). |
| **High/Very High additional requirements** | Layered requirements beyond the ISO baseline, depending on the objectives (labels) you're assessed against. |
| **Labels + ENX exchange mechanics** | Results are shared as labels on the ENX platform, with a 9-month corrective-action window for nonconformities and 3-year assessment cycles — not an ISO-style certificate with annual surveillance audits. |
| **OEM/automotive expectations** | Automotive-specific data-classification handling per VW's terms; TISAX is the mechanism OEMs actually require contractually. |

## Version status (applies to your planning)

- The **current catalogue is VDA ISA 6** (revision 6.0.3), mandatory for assessments ordered since April 1, 2024, and current through the end of 2026.
- **ISA2027** was published July 1, 2026, and becomes **mandatory for assessments ordered from January 1, 2027** (44 of 46 Information Security controls edited, prototype module restructured, updated ISO 27001:2022/NIST CSF 2.0 mappings).
- Since today is August 23, 2026, an assessment you **order this year runs on ISA 6**; ordering **from January 1, 2027 puts you on ISA2027**. Worth confirming with VW/your audit provider which timeline they expect, and locking your order date deliberately.

## How much extra work — realistically less than a first-timer

Being ISO 27001:2022-certified is **the best possible starting point** and will make this materially faster than for an organization starting from zero. The practical workflow:

1. **Map your existing ISO 27001 evidence** (policies, risk register, Statement of Applicability, internal/external audit artifacts) to the ISA Information Security control areas — most of it transfers directly given the ~70–80% overlap. ISA2027 ships updated ISO 27001:2022 mappings if you land on that catalogue version.
2. **Gap-assess the unmapped ISA content**: any prototype module requirements (if VW's objectives require Proto/Test Vehicle labels), additional High/Very High requirements tied to the confidentiality/availability labels VW is asking for, and availability-specific objectives.
3. **Re-express your ISO evidence in TISAX maturity terms** — "implemented" under ISO doesn't automatically mean maturity 3 under TISAX; it needs to be established, documented, and demonstrably operating to hit that target, and the cutback rule means you can't lean on strengths to cover weak spots.
4. **Run TISAX as a scope extension of your existing ISMS**, not a parallel compliance program — you want one ISMS serving both.
5. Still required regardless of ISO status: **ENX portal registration** (€405 per location/scope, or €5,000/year flat fee, + VAT), **standard scope** selection, an **ENX-accredited audit provider**, and the formal **AL2 or AL3 assessment** (level depends on which objectives/labels VW requires — confirm this with VW, as it wasn't specified). Nonconformities go through the standard **corrective action plan process** with a 9-month follow-up window, and labels are valid **3 years**.

## Bottom line
Your ISO 27001:2022 certification significantly shortens the runway — expect a gap-assessment and evidence-remapping exercise rather than building an ISMS from scratch — but you still need to register with ENX, select an accredited audit provider, and complete the formal TISAX assessment (AL2 or AL3, depending on VW's required objectives) to get labels VW can accept. Confirm with VW exactly which labels/objectives they require, since that determines your assessment level and how much of the prototype/availability content applies.

*This is general compliance information, not legal advice. Confirm the specific objectives/labels VW requires and current ENX fees directly with VW and an accredited audit provider.*
