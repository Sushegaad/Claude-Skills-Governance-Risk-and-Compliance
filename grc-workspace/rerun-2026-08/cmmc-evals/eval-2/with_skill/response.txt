## Prioritized Remediation Plan

1. **Deploy MFA organization-wide** (IA.L2-3.5.3) — extend beyond cloud email to VPN, domain logon, and all CUI-touching applications. Highest score impact, critical/non-negotiable for certification.
2. **Segment CUI off the flat network** (AC.L2-3.1.3, SC.L2-3.13.16, MP.L2-3.8.1/3.8.2) — move CUI off open shared drives into an access-controlled, encrypted enclave. This single project resolves several findings at once.
3. **Implement FIPS-validated encryption** at rest and verify in transit (SC.L2-3.13.11, SC.L2-3.13.8) — replace or reconfigure crypto modules; obtain FIPS 140-2/3 validation certificates as evidence.
4. **Confirm and harden audit logging** (AU.L2-3.3.1) — this is unaddressed in your facts and is critical; treat as urgent until verified.
5. **Update the SSP** (CA.L2-3.12.4) — refresh to reflect current environment; this is also the document that will host your POA&M references for everything above.
6. **Test the incident response plan** (IR.L2-3.6.1, IR.L2-3.6.3) — run a tabletop exercise and document results; relatively low cost, meaningful score and posture improvement.
7. **Verify and close smaller gaps**: insider-threat training content, personnel offboarding process, least-privilege enforcement on remaining systems.

**Important caveat**: IA.L2-3.5.3, AC.L2-3.1.3, SC.L2-3.13.8, SC.L2-3.13.11, and (pending verification) AU.L2-3.3.1 and IR.L2-3.6.1 are all on the **critical-practices list that can never be POA&M-eligible at certification** — these must be fully remediated, not just documented as "in progress," before you can pursue certification of any kind.
