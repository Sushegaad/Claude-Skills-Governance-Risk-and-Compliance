# ISO 27001:2022 vs NCA ECC-2:2024 — Coverage and Remaining Gaps

A couple of confirmations that would sharpen the gap list — I've proceeded on stated assumptions:

- You said "Saudi government subsidiary," so **NCA ECC-2:2024 is mandatory** for you outright (government entities and their subsidiaries are always ECC-covered, no CNI test needed).
- I'm assuming your ISO 27001:2022 certification is current, covers the same organizational/system scope as the ECC assessment will need to cover, and that you haven't yet run a formal ECC self-assessment.
- I'm assuming you also process Saudi residents' personal data (near-universal for any operating entity) and use cloud infrastructure to some degree — both add obligations beyond ECC itself (see matrix).

## Applicability Matrix

| Instrument | Regulator | Applies to you? |
|---|---|---|
| **NCA ECC-2:2024** | NCA | **Yes — mandatory.** Government subsidiaries are always ECC-covered. |
| **NCA CCC** (Cloud Cybersecurity Controls) | NCA | **Yes, if you use cloud** — tenant-side controls, since you're an ECC-covered entity. Confirm your cloud footprint. |
| **NCA DCC-1:2022** (Data Cybersecurity Controls) | NCA | **Likely yes** — data-lifecycle controls for ECC-covered entities. |
| **NCA OTCC-1:2022 / TCC-1:2021 / CSCC-1:2019** | NCA | **Conditional** — only if you operate OT/ICS environments, telework at scale, or critical systems respectively. Flag if any apply; otherwise out of scope here. |
| **Saudi PDPL** | SDAIA | **Yes — mandatory**, independent of ECC, for any personal data you process. |
| **ISO 27001:2022** | (voluntary certification, not a Saudi legal instrument) | Your existing certification — valuable as evidence and control-maturity signal, but it does **not** substitute for NCA's ECC self-assessment/reporting obligation. |

**The core point:** ISO 27001:2022 certification is strong *evidence* for many ECC controls, but it does not satisfy the ECC compliance obligation itself. NCA requires its own self-assessment against ECC's specific control set, submitted through NCA's compliance tool, with NCA audit/on-site assessment rights — a third-party ISO certificate is supporting evidence, not a substitute filing.

## What specifically remains, beyond the domain table

1. **Formal ECC self-assessment and NCA submission** — your ISO audit trail doesn't automatically populate this; you need a control-by-control ECC self-assessment (108 main controls + 92 subcontrols) with evidence mapped to NCA's rating scale, submitted via NCA's compliance tool, and be prepared for NCA audit/on-site assessment.
2. **Arabic-language governance artifacts** where NCA requires them — confirm which policy/procedure documents need Arabic versions for NCA review.
3. **PDPL registration and program**, entirely separate from ECC — SDAIA National Data Governance Platform registration, RoPA, DPO assessment, breach notification procedure (72-hour SDAIA clock). ISO 27001 doesn't cover this at all; it's a parallel legal obligation, not a subset of ECC.
4. **NCA CCC tenant-side controls and cloud residency confirmation** if you use any cloud services — this is the biggest structural gap ISO doesn't touch.
5. **OT/ICS, telework, and critical-systems controls** (OTCC/TCC/CSCC) if any of those environments exist in your operations — these sit outside ECC-2:2024 itself (ICS was moved out of ECC-2 into standalone OTCC-1:2022) and outside ISO 27001 entirely; confirm applicability.
6. **Resilience domain depth** — likely your single biggest content gap versus ISO, since ECC treats cybersecurity resilience as a full domain while ISO 27001:2022 covers it more lightly.
