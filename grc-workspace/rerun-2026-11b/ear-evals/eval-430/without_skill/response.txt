The BIS Export Compliance Guidelines: The Elements of an Effective Export Compliance Program (2017) have eight elements:

1. Management commitment
2. Risk assessment
3. Export authorization (authorization, classification and screening)
4. Recordkeeping
5. Training
6. Audits
7. Handling export violations and taking corrective actions
8. Compliance manual (the written Export Compliance Program manual)

Note that some summaries label the elements slightly differently. The 2017 guidelines list these eight. The earlier BIS Export Management and Compliance Program (EMCP) had a different structure.
