ISO/IEC 27701:2019 had two normative control annexes: Annex A (PIMS-specific reference control objectives and controls for PII controllers) with 31 controls, and Annex B (for PII processors) with 18 controls, so 49 PII-specific controls in total, in addition to the ISO/IEC 27002 additions in clauses 5-6.

The 2025 edition (published in 2025) is a standalone standard and no longer an extension of ISO/IEC 27001/27002. It restructures the content into its own management system clauses plus a consolidated control annex, with controls for controllers and processors in one place and cross-references to ISO/IEC 27002:2022. The exact count and one-to-one mapping I cannot state with confidence from memory. In general terms, many 2019 controls carry over (often reworded and renumbered, for example under A.1.x groupings such as conditions for collection and processing, obligations to PII principals, privacy by design and default, and PII sharing, transfer and disclosure), some controller and processor controls are merged, and some are new or clarified. Please check the official mapping table in the standard (or ISO's transition guidance) before relying on a specific number.
