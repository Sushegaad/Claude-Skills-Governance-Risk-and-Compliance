In ISO/IEC 27701:2019, temporary files were control 7.4.6 (Temporary files), under 7.4 Privacy by design and by default, for controllers. Processors had a corresponding control B.8.4.1 "Temporary files"... (the processor annex covers it in B.8.4 on privacy by design and default).

In 2025, the privacy by design and default group is A.1.4.x, so the control is likely in A.1.4.x with a number close to the old one (possibly A.1.4.6), but I cannot confirm the exact number from memory. Check the mapping table in the 2025 edition.
