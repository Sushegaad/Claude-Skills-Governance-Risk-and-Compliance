Below are (1) what I can state with reasonable confidence, (2) a method for building the briefing accurately, and (3) a ready-to-fill briefing template.

1. Things I can say with reasonable confidence (context, not 2026 news)
- PCI DSS v4.0.1 is the active version. v4.0 was retired December 31, 2024, and all future-dated requirements became mandatory March 31, 2025.
- PA-DSS was retired in 2022. The Software Security Framework (Secure Software and Secure SLC standards) is the replacement.
- SPoC and CPoC are being replaced by MPoC. My understanding is that the SPoC and CPoC listings reach their sunset around October 31, 2026. Verify this.
- The Council has published AI-related guidance, including how assessors should use AI in assessments. Verify any 2026 updates.
- The Council usually publishes on a rolling basis: standards revisions, FAQs, guidelines, information supplements, assessor program updates (QSA Qualification Requirements and QSA program guide), forms and templates (ROC, AOC, SAQ), and lists of validated products and solutions.

2. How to compile the accurate list (about half a day)
- PCI SSC Press Releases and Blog (July to December 2026).
- Document Library, filtered by "recently updated". Check the Standards, Supporting Documents, Programs, and Guidance categories.
- FAQs (Knowledge Base), sorted by date. Check for new or revised FAQs.
- Assessor-facing portal and QSA/ISA/AQSA updates and webinars: Assessor Quality Management (AQM) changes, QSA Program Guide, Qualification Requirements, and new templates and training.
- Lists of Validated Products and Solutions, and any listing expiries and retirements (Secure SLC, Secure Software, SPoC/CPoC/MPoC, P2PE, PTS, PIN, 3DS).
- Standard-specific pages: PCI DSS, PCI PIN, P2PE, 3DS Core and SDK, Card Production, Software Security Framework, MPoC.
- Council's published dates for Global Community Meetings and webinars.
- Acquirer and payment brand bulletins (Visa, Mastercard, etc.), which often act on the Council's changes.

3. Briefing template for the QSA-facing team
For each item record: Title | Date | Type (standard, FAQ, guidance, program, template, listing) | What changed | Effective date or sunset | Who is affected | Action | Owner | Source link.

Section headers to use:
A. Standards and versions (PCI DSS, PCI SSF, MPoC, PIN, P2PE, 3DS, PTS)
B. Sunsets and retirements (SPoC/CPoC, older SSF versions, device listings)
C. Guidance and supplements (AI, cloud, third-party, e-commerce, etc.)
D. Assessor program changes (QSA qualification requirements, QA, templates, reporting instructions)
E. FAQ changes that affect how we scope, test, or evidence
F. Impact on our clients and our engagements (scoping questions, evidence requests, report wording)
G. Open questions to confirm with the Council or our QSA company's PCI liaison
H. Actions, owners, and dates

4. Talking points that stay true regardless
- Baseline is PCI DSS v4.0.1 unless a newer version is verified.
- Validate any third-party listing (status and expiry) on the PCI SSC site before relying on it in a ROC.
- Check effective dates for any new document: "published" is not "mandatory".
- Cite the source document and version in your working papers.
