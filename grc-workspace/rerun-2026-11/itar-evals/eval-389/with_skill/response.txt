1. Omnibus USML review (PROPOSED rule, not final)
- "Review of the U.S. Munitions List and Related Definitions and License Exemptions," 91 FR 62361, published October 1, 2026, docket DOS-2026-0991. Comments are due November 30, 2026.
- Proposes: removal of VIII(h)(10) (most radar altimeters off the USML); narrowing of II(a)(4) and X(a)(7); restructuring of XX(c); a new 22 CFR 120.35 for items excluded from the USML; two new 22 CFR 120.41 "specially designed" releases; and a new 123.26 exemption for temporary export of foreign defense articles for OEM servicing and repair.
- Status: proposals only. Apply current rules until a final rule issues.

2. Category XI(b) temporary modification extended
- The temporary modification, which retains "analyze and produce information from" and "software," is extended through August 30, 2028 (91 FR 55461).

3. DECCS Data Collection Modernization
- The licensing release went live on DECCS on September 14, 2026. Licensing now runs on the updated forms, so check your submission procedures and templates.

4. Cyprus
- DDTC suspended Cyprus's 22 CFR 126.1 proscribed status through September 30, 2027 (91 FR 57787, September 11, 2026). Check the current 126.1 list before any Cyprus transaction.

5. Enforcement: BAE Systems, Inc. consent agreement (August 2026)
- $36M consent agreement covering 104 AECA/ITAR violations from May 2019 to March 2025, which were voluntarily disclosed. They include 38 unauthorized technical data exports to China (a 126.1 country) and unauthorized exports and defense services involving six other countries.
- Terms: $18M suspended for remedial compliance spending, a 3-year agreement, a Special Compliance Officer, and an external audit. No debarment, reflecting credit for voluntary disclosure and cooperation.
- Lessons: weak written procedures, inexperienced staff, poor agreement management and no system for tracking authorizations drive penalties.

6. The aircraft survivability rule, for context
- 91 FR 55457 (August 28, 2026; correction 91 FR 58610), an interim final rule effective October 13, 2026. It excludes certain survivability-enhanced civil aircraft from VIII(a)(8). ASE stays under XI(a)(4).
