Under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP, basic and sensitive personal data were listed in Article 2 (definitions). Under the new PDPL (Law 91/2025/QH15, effective 1 January 2026), the definitions are in the law's definitions article and the Government is delegated to issue detailed lists, so the precise lists sit in implementing decrees. I am not certain of the exact article numbers.

Are the lists closed? Not entirely. The sensitive data category is defined as data tied to individual privacy that, if violated, directly affects legitimate rights and interests. The Decree 13 list was a specific enumeration (political and religious views, health records, race, genetic and biometric data, sex life, criminal records, financial and bank data, location data, etc.) with a catch-all category for other data prescribed by law as specific and needing security measures. The new law and its decrees may add categories, so treat the lists as enumerated but updatable by the Government, not as permanently closed. Check the current implementing decree before relying on them.
