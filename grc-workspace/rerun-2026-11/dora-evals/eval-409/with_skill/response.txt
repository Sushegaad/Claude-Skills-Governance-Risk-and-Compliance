All in Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA), Chapter V, Section I.

Termination rights
- Art. 28(7): financial entities must ensure ICT service arrangements can be terminated in the circumstances DORA requires.
- Art. 30(2) baseline contractual elements (a)-(i), applying to all ICT contracts, include termination rights and minimum notice periods, plus data portability, return and migration assistance on termination.
- The detail is in CDR (EU) 2024/1773.

Exit strategies
- Art. 28(8): an exit strategy is required for every arrangement supporting a critical or important function. It covers triggers, the notice and migration period, data portability and return, and transition assistance. It should be tested periodically on a risk basis.
- Art. 30(3)(f): for contracts supporting critical or important functions, the contract must contain exit strategies with a mandatory transition period.
- Art. 30(4): review of exit viability before renewal.
- The Register of Information (CIR (EU) 2024/2956) carries an exit strategy reference per arrangement.

Concentration risk
- Art. 28(4)(c): the pre-contractual assessment includes ICT concentration risk. This is where the entity-level duty sits.
- Art. 29: preliminary assessment of ICT concentration risk at entity level. It covers concentration in a single provider, the risk of ICT services becoming unavailable, and assessment before new arrangements for critical functions. It is transaction-triggered and complements Art. 28(4)(c).
- Systemic concentration is dealt with through CTPP designation and oversight, Art. 31-44.

Related points
- Art. 28(3): the Register of Information is maintained and submitted at least annually.
- Art. 28(6): audit and inspection frequency.
- Art. 28(1): the ICT third-party risk policy must address concentration, exit and the contract lifecycle.
- Practical tip: rate each provider on dependency depth, substitutability, contractual exit, financial stability, geographic diversification and enforceability of audit rights. Two or more High ratings should trigger mitigation.
