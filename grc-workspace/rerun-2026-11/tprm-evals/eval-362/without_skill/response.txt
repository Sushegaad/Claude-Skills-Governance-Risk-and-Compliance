Below is the verified baseline, what could plausibly have moved, and how to check quickly.

1. Baseline (verified through my knowledge)
- Cross-Industry Guidance on Outsourcing (CBI, published December 2021; firms had until 14 December 2022 to comply). It covers governance and board accountability, outsourcing policy and strategy, risk assessment, due diligence, contractual provisions, ongoing monitoring, sub-outsourcing (fourth parties), concentration risk, intragroup arrangements, exit strategies and business continuity, and notification to the CBI of planned or actual critical or important outsourcing. It is guidance, but the CBI expects firms to apply it and measures them against it, alongside the Corporate Governance Requirements, the Cross-Industry Guidance on Operational Resilience (2021, in force from December 2023), and IT/cyber risk guidance (2016 Cross-Industry Guidance on IT Outsourcing and cyber).
- The CBI's Outsourcing Register / outsourcing notification expectations, and its 2022 Dear CEO and thematic findings on outsourcing.
- DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554) has applied since 17 January 2025 to most CBI-regulated financial entities (banks, investment firms, insurers, payment and e-money institutions, UCITS ManCos/AIFMs, etc.). For ICT third-party arrangements, DORA Chapter V, the RTS on ICT policy and subcontracting, the Register of Information (first submissions 2025) and ITS templates take precedence. The CBI has stated that its outsourcing guidance continues to apply to non-ICT outsourcing and to entities outside DORA's scope, and where it does not conflict with DORA. Entities in scope of EBA Guidelines on outsourcing (EBA/GL/2019/02) and EIOPA/ESMA outsourcing guidelines (cloud) must also consider those.
