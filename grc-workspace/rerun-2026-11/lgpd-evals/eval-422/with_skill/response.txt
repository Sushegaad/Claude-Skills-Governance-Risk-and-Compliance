LGPD (Law 13,709/2018, as amended by Law 13,853/2019) chapter map, with the two locations you asked about first.

Where termination of processing sits
- Chapter II (Processing of Personal Data), Section IV, "Término do Tratamento de Dados": Art. 15 defines the events on which processing terminates, and Art. 16 requires elimination of the data after termination, subject to the retention exceptions listed there. Art. 18, VI (deletion of consent-based data) cross-refers to Art. 16.

Where the ANPD sits
- Chapter IX (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados and Conselho Nacional de Proteção de Dados Pessoais e da Privacidade), starting at Art. 55-A. Art. 55-A establishes the ANPD, and the competencies are in Art. 55-J.
- Law 15.352/2026 (DOU, 25 February 2026, converting MP 1.317/2025) turned the ANPD into an autarquia de regime especial with functional, decision-making and financial autonomy.
- Its sanctioning power (Arts. 52-54) is in Chapter VIII, not Chapter IX.

Note on section grouping: this reference groups Arts. 11-13 (sensitive data, anonymised data, research) as a sub-part of Chapter II. Art. 14 (children and adolescents) follows, then Section IV. If you cite by section in a formal document, confirm the official Planalto text, because the section numbering inside Chapter II is easy to misquote.

Quick pointers
- Legal bases: Art. 7 (ten bases) and Art. 11 (sensitive).
- Rights: Art. 18. Breach notice: Art. 48 (3 working days preliminary under Resolution CD/ANPD 15/2024, 20 working days full report).
- Transfers: Arts. 33-36. EU/EEA is adequate since January 2026 (Resolution CD/ANPD 32/2026).
- Sanctions: Art. 52 (fines up to 2% of Brazilian revenue, R$50 million per violation). Art. 54 covers the criteria for the daily fine.
