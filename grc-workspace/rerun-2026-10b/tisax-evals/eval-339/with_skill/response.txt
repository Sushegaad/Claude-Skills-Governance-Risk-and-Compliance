Good news: TISAX is a well-defined process, and a first-timer typically needs about 6 to 12 months to reach consistent maturity 3, depending on the ISMS baseline. First, the framing: TISAX is an assessment with shareable labels, not a certification. It is run by the ENX Association on behalf of the VDA, and one assessment can be shared with many OEM customers ("assess once, share with many").

**Step 0: Ask your customer exactly what they need**
Get the required assessment objectives (labels) in writing, plus the deadline. The objectives set the level:
- Confidential, High Availability, Data: AL2 (audit provider plausibility check, usually remote).
- Strictly Confidential, Very High Availability, prototype labels, Special Data: AL3 (comprehensive on-site assessment).
- AL1 is self-assessment only. It gives no labels and no ENX visibility, so it is useful internally but will not satisfy your customer.
Use current label names. "Info High" and "Info Very High" are legacy names, replaced under ISA 6 by Confidential/Strictly Confidential plus the availability labels.

**Step 1: Version decision**
ISA 6 (rev. 6.0.3) applies to assessments ordered through December 31, 2026. ISA2027 applies to orders from January 1, 2027. An initial ISA-6 assessment must open by March 2027. Since you are starting from zero, you will probably be ready after the changeover, so build your gap assessment against ISA2027.

**Step 2: Register on the ENX portal and define scope**
- Register your legal entity and location(s).
- Use the standard scope, which is strongly recommended. Custom scopes are less widely accepted.

**Step 3: Gap assessment and remediation**
Run a self-assessment against the ISA catalogue (Information Security, 46 controls; plus Prototype Protection or Data Protection if your objectives include them). Score each question on the 0-5 maturity scale. The target is maturity 3 ("Established"). Cutback rules trim scores above target, so weak areas drag the result down and strengths elsewhere do not offset them. Aim for a consistent 3 before chasing 4 or 5 anywhere. Sequence the work as must requirements, then should requirements, then the additional requirements for high and very-high protection needs.

Typical first-timer gaps: ISMS governance and documented policies, asset classification, access reviews, logging and vulnerability management, supplier management, incident process, and evidence that processes actually operate.

**Step 4: Select an ENX-accredited audit provider**
Book early, since provider capacity drives timelines. Agree objectives and level.

**Step 5: Assessment**
Kick-off, self-assessment submission, then the initial assessment (remote for AL2, on-site for AL3). If there are nonconformities, you submit a corrective action plan, may receive temporary labels, and have a follow-up assessment within the 9-month corrective window (counted from the initial assessment's closing meeting). Miss that window and the assessment restarts.

**Step 6: Labels and sharing**
Labels are valid for 3 years. You share results with customers on the ENX platform and choose the disclosure detail (label only, summary, or full report). Results are never public by default. Renewal is a full re-assessment against the then-current catalogue.

**Cost**
1. **ENX fees (plus VAT):** €405 net per location and scope (one-time, Assessment Based Charges), or €5,000 per year for Participation Based Charges covering unlimited locations and scopes. For one or two sites the per-scope option is cheaper. The flat fee suits many sites.
2. **Audit provider fees:** these are separate, set by the provider, and usually the largest external cost. They depend on level (AL3 on-site costs more than AL2), number of locations, and company size. Get quotes from several accredited providers; I will not quote a figure because it varies widely.
3. **Internal and consulting cost:** time for remediation, tooling (for example logging, MDM, vulnerability scanning) and optional consulting. For a first-timer this is often the largest total.

**Next steps**
1. Confirm objectives, level and deadline with your customer.
2. Decide the ISA 6 versus ISA2027 order date.
3. Register on ENX and fix the scope.
4. Run the gap assessment and build a remediation roadmap.
5. Request quotes from accredited providers.
