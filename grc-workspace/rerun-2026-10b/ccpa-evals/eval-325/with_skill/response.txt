Here is the timeline as of October 3, 2026. The regulations were finalized in 2025 and took effect January 1, 2026, but several obligations phase in later.

**Timeline at a glance**

| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Jan 1, 2026 | ADMT, risk-assessment, and cybersecurity-audit regulations are effective. Risk assessments are required for new significant-risk processing from this date. |
| Jan 1, 2027 | Compliance deadline for ADMT used to make significant decisions: pre-use notice, opt-out, and access rights. |
| Dec 31, 2027 | Deadline to complete risk assessments for processing that was already ongoing as of Jan 1, 2026. |
| Apr 1, 2028 | First risk-assessment submissions to the CPPA. First cybersecurity-audit certification tier due. |
| Apr 2029 and Apr 2030 | Later cybersecurity-audit certification tiers, by revenue band. |

**1. Risk assessments (live since January 1, 2026)**

- Before you begin processing that presents significant risk to consumers, you must conduct and document a risk assessment. Typical triggers are selling or sharing PI, processing sensitive PI, and using ADMT for significant decisions.
- Assessments weigh benefits against risks to consumers and identify safeguards. The CPPA can request them at any time.
- For processing already underway on January 1, 2026, you have until **December 31, 2027** to complete the assessment. Submissions to the CPPA begin by **April 1, 2028**.
- Practical point: new projects launched now need the assessment before launch. Do not assume the 2027 date protects them.

**2. ADMT (deadline January 1, 2027)**

- ADMT regulations cover technology used to make or substantially drive decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, such as employment, credit, housing, insurance, and education.
- By **January 1, 2027** you must provide:
  - a **pre-use notice** explaining the use and purpose,
  - an **opt-out mechanism**, or a qualifying human-review alternative,
  - **access rights**, meaning information about the logic and how outputs were used, and a path to human review.
- Mind the sequencing: the ADMT rules apply to significant decisions from January 1, 2027, but the risk assessment for that ADMT use is a separate duty with its own date above.

**3. Cybersecurity audits (phased)**

- Businesses whose processing presents significant security risk must conduct **annual cybersecurity audits**. The regulations took effect January 1, 2026.
- Certification of completion is due to the CPPA on a revenue-tiered schedule: **April 2028, April 2029, and April 2030**. The largest businesses go first.
- Audits must be performed by a qualified, objective reviewer and cover the controls the regulations list. Retain the work papers.

**Related 2026 context**

- Consumer-request deadlines in the broker context are tightening (AB 883), and SB 923 takes effect January 1, 2027. Both interact with your ADMT and data inventory work.
- Enforcement is active. Disney ($2.75M), GM ($12.75M), PlayOn ($1.1M), and Ford ($375K) show systematic and deadline failures draw penalties of up to $2,500 per unintentional and $7,500 per intentional violation.

**Recommended workplan**

1. **Now to Q4 2026:** inventory every automated system that influences decisions about employment, credit, housing, insurance, or education. Classify which are in scope for ADMT.
2. **Q4 2026:** draft the pre-use notice, the opt-out and human-review workflow, and the access-response template. Test the intake channel and its 15-business-day and 45-day clocks.
3. **Now onward:** gate new significant-risk projects behind a documented risk assessment.
4. **During 2027:** complete assessments for legacy processing before December 31, 2027, and prepare the submission package.
5. **Plan for audits:** pick an auditor and a control framework, and set the first certification date from your revenue tier.
6. **Governance:** assign owners, keep records (24 months is the minimum if you handle PI of 10 million or more consumers), and brief your board.

Check the final regulation text and the CPPA's current guidance for exact scoping and the revenue tiers before committing to a date. This is general compliance information, not legal advice.
