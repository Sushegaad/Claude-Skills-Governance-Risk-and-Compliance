# TISAX Objectives, Labels & Assessment Levels

## The 12 assessment objectives / labels (ISA 6 era)

| Label | Introduced | Protection need | Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Confidential** | ISA 6 (replaces part of Info High) | High confidentiality | AL2 |
| **Strictly Confidential** | ISA 6 (replaces part of Info Very High) | Very high confidentiality | AL3 |
| **High Availability** | ISA 6 (split from Info High) | High availability | AL2 |
| **Very High Availability** | ISA 6 (split from Info Very High) | Very high availability | AL3 |
| Info High / Info Very High | Legacy | Superseded naming — holders were auto-granted the new confidentiality labels | — |
| **Proto Parts** / **Proto Vehicles** / **Test Vehicles** / **Proto Events** | Prototype catalogue | Physical prototype protection | AL3 (physical labels) |
| **Data** | Data Protection catalogue | GDPR Art. 28 processing | AL2 |
| **Special Data** | Data Protection catalogue | GDPR Art. 9 special categories | AL3 |

ISA2027 introduces a restructured **PTS** prototype-label structure for assessments ordered from 2027.

## Assessment levels
- **AL1** — self-assessment only; no verification, no labels, no ENX visibility. Internal readiness use.
- **AL2** — audit-provider plausibility check: reviewed self-assessment + evidence + interviews, usually remote.
- **AL3** — comprehensive on-site assessment: evidence, interviews, physical inspection.
The chosen objectives determine the level — not the other way round.

## Validity & sharing
Labels are valid **3 years** from assessment. Results live on the **ENX platform**; the participant controls which partners see them and at what detail level (label-only → summary → full report). Results are never public by default. Renewal = full re-assessment against the then-current catalogue.
