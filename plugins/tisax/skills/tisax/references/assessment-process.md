# TISAX Assessment Process — ENX Portal to Labels

## 1. Registration (ENX portal)
Register the legal entity and location(s). Fees: **€405 net per location and scope** (one-time, Assessment Based Charges) OR the **€5,000/year Participation Based Charges** flat fee (unlimited locations/scopes), plus German VAT where applicable.

## 2. Scoping
- **Standard scope** (strongly recommended): comparable and accepted by all partners
- Custom scopes (full/extended) reduce acceptance — use only with a concrete partner agreement
- Scope = the locations, processes, and IT that handle the partner's protectable information

## 3. Audit provider & objectives
Select an **ENX-accredited audit provider**; agree objectives (which fix the assessment level and catalogue modules). Book early — provider capacity drives timelines.

## 4. Assessment phases
1. Kick-off and self-assessment submission (AL2/AL3)
2. **Initial assessment** (remote plausibility for AL2; on-site for AL3)
3. Nonconformities → **corrective action plan assessment** (plan reviewed by the provider); **temporary labels** may be issued
4. **Follow-up assessment(s)** verifying remediation — all within the **9-month corrective window** (temporary labels expire 9 months after the initial assessment's closing meeting; miss the window and the assessment restarts)
5. Final report → **labels issued (3-year validity)** → share via ENX platform

## Readiness guidance
- Typical first-timer runway: 6–12 months to consistent maturity 3, depending on ISMS baseline (ISO 27001 holders are materially faster)
- Sequence: must requirements → should requirements → High/Very-High additional requirements → prototype/data modules if in objectives
- Evidence discipline mirrors ISO audits: policies with approval trails, operational records, technical configuration exports, interview-ready owners
- **Order-date decision (2026/27)**: ordering in 2026 locks ISA 6; from January 1, 2027 assessments run on ISA2027 — plan the gap assessment against the catalogue you will actually face
