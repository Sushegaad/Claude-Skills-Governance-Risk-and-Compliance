# VDA ISA Catalogue — Structure & Versions

## ISA 6 (current; revision 6.0.3) — mandatory for assessments ordered since April 1, 2024, current through 2026

**Information Security catalogue (46 controls)** — core module every TISAX assessment includes. Control areas span: IS policies & organization, human resources, physical security & business environment, identity & access management, IT security/cryptography, operations security (incl. malware, logging, vulnerability management), system acquisition & development, supplier relationships, incident management, and business continuity. Requirements at the base level carry additional layered requirements for **High** and **Very High** protection needs (~297 requirements at High; ~17 further at Very High per consultancy analyses).

**Prototype Protection catalogue (22 controls)** — physical and organizational protection for prototype parts, vehicles, events: perimeter/zone security, camouflage and transport rules, test-drive protections, film/photo controls, personnel commitments.

**Data Protection catalogue (4 controls)** — GDPR Art. 28 processor posture: DPA hygiene, instructions handling, sub-processor management, support for controller obligations. The Special Data objective layers Art. 9 special-category handling.

**Requirement tiers**: "must" = mandatory; "should" = expected unless justified with equivalent risk treatment; additional requirements activate with High/Very High objectives. Assessment covers implementation AND maturity.

## ISA2027 — published July 1, 2026; mandatory for assessments ordered from January 1, 2027

- Year-based naming replaces version numbers; annual release cadence begins
- 44 of 46 Information Security controls edited/clarified
- Prototype module restructured: 20 controls in 2 domains; new PTS prototype-label structure
- Updated mappings to ISO 27001:2022 and NIST CSF 2.0
- Transition rule: the catalogue version is fixed by the assessment ORDER date — order in 2026 → ISA 6; order from Jan 1, 2027 → ISA2027

## Citation discipline
Cite ISA control numbers only when confident of the specific control in the official ISA document; otherwise cite the control area by name and direct users to the current VDA ISA download (vda.de / enx.com). Never invent control numbers.

## ISA2027 Detail (published in full July 1, 2026; ISA2027.1 current)
Redline vs ISA 6.0.1 and webinar series available from ENX since August 7, 2026. Key changes: annual year-based release cycle (summer publication, January 1 effectiveness); IS module stays at 46 controls with ~44 edited and substantive requirement changes in ~25 (counts per consultancy analyses of the workbook); formalized "the following aspects are considered" semantics (each aspect consciously considered and explainable); broader Project definition; clearer event-vs-incident distinction; supply-chain strengthening (high: documented supplier monitoring; very high: TISAX label / equivalent third-party assessment / supplier audit); prototype module 22→20 controls in two domains (Organizational Requirements; Physical & Environmental Security) with new traceability/lifecycle and disposal/recycling/return controls; PTS labels Prototype Protection Basic (AL2) / Facilities (AL3); mappings to NIST CSF 2.0 and refined ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (all 2013 references removed). Transition: mandatory for orders from January 1, 2027; final date to OPEN an initial ISA-6 assessment is March 2027; labels keep validity.
