# TPRM — Claude Skill

**File:** `tprm.skill`

Turns Claude into an expert **third-party risk management (TPRM) analyst** — the recurring vendor-risk discipline every framework in this collection requires (ISO 27001 A.5.19–5.23, SOC 2 CC9.2, NIS2 Art. 21(2)(d), DORA Chapter V, HIPAA BAAs, GDPR Art. 28).

## What it covers
- **Vendor tiering** by data access, criticality and substitutability (interagency-guidance lifecycle: planning → due diligence → contracting → monitoring → termination)
- **Due-diligence questionnaires** — tailored, SIG-Lite-style, framework-mapped per question (SIG 2026 landscape awareness without reproducing licensed content)
- **SOC 2 Type II report review** — opinion and period checks, exception triage, carve-out vs inclusive subservice analysis, CUEC extraction, CSOCs, bridge-letter rules
- **DPA & sub-processor review** (GDPR Art. 28 clause checklist; BAA vs DPA distinctions), **contract security addenda** (DORA Art. 30 provisions for critical functions)
- **Ongoing monitoring** signals incl. OAuth/integration blast radius (Salesloft Drift, Shai-Hulud lessons) and a verification-driven **offboarding checklist**

## What it does
Tier models with per-tier due-diligence depth · framework-cited questionnaire generation · SOC 2 findings memos with CUEC ownership checklists · clause-by-clause DPA reviews · tier-scaled contract addenda · monitoring and offboarding checklists with owners.
